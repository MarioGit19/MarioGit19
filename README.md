## <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide/terminal.svg?color=%232DD4BF&height=26" align="top" />&nbsp; About Me


I'm a **Full Stack Developer at Shop Circle**, building and maintaining production Shopify
applications on the **Releasit Apps** team — products trusted by thousands of merchants worldwide.
I work across the entire lifecycle: shaping the architecture, crafting the storefront experience,
engineering the backend services, and owning the cloud infrastructure and observability that keep
everything running reliably at scale.

I value pragmatic engineering and clean, maintainable code — solutions built to solve real
problems for real users. I've architected and shipped complete applications solo, and I'm equally
at home in a React frontend, a Node/Nest.js backend, or a Dockerized deployment on AWS and Hetzner.

- <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide/puzzle.svg?color=%232DD4BF&height=17" align="top" />&nbsp; **Design, architect and ship** Shopify apps end-to-end — one taken from concept to App Store in under **6 weeks**, solo.
- <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide/wrench.svg?color=%232DD4BF&height=17" align="top" />&nbsp; **Full-stack range** — React, Next.js, Remix & React Native on the front; Node, Nest.js, GraphQL & REST APIs on the back.
- <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide/cloud.svg?color=%232DD4BF&height=17" align="top" />&nbsp; **Own the infrastructure** — AWS, Hetzner, Docker and Supabase, with Sentry & CloudWatch for observability.
- <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide/store.svg?color=%232DD4BF&height=17" align="top" />&nbsp; **Deep in the Shopify ecosystem** — Liquid, Polaris, App Bridge and the Storefront API.
- <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide/gamepad-2.svg?color=%232DD4BF&height=17" align="top" />&nbsp; **Off the clock** — I build Lua addons for World of Warcraft and automate anything I'd rather not do twice.
